Descripción

The world needs innovations, and our team needs you!

BASF is the world's leading chemical company #WeCreateChemistry and one of the best places to work and develop your career. We are looking for diverse, passionate, and creative talent who wants to make a difference. BASF Española S.L. markets more than 5,000 products to meet the needs of our customers and society across a wide variety of sectors.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

As a member of BASF's Global FCC Technical Service team, you will play a critical role in maximizing customer value, refinery performance, and business growth across our FCC catalyst portfolio. Acting as a trusted advisor to customers and a key link between commercial, technical, manufacturing, and innovation teams, you will drive technical excellence, strengthen customer partnerships, and help shape the future of FCC technology solutions.

  • Play a critical role BASF's FCC Technical Service team, providing expert support on catalyst performance, additive applications, troubleshooting, and refinery unit optimization to deliver outstanding customer outcomes.
  • Build trusted relationships with refinery customers by leading technical reviews, site visits, startups, turnarounds, and performance assessments, while serving as a key technical advisor.
  • Partner with Commercial, R&D, Manufacturing, Product Management, and Global Technology teams to support customer retention, drive business growth, and successfully develop and commercialize new solutions.
  • Deliver technical excellence through catalyst evaluations, refinery data analysis, and resolution of complex FCC operating challenges, ensuring recommendations align with both customer and business objectives.
  • Drive operational effectiveness by supporting technical projects, resource planning, customer deliverables, and continuous improvements to processes, tools, safety, quality, and regulatory compliance.
  • Liaise with industry experts, licensors, laboratories, and consultants to ensure high levels of service delivery to our customers

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

  • Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD in Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Materials Science, or a related technical discipline.
  • Significant experience in refining, FCC operations, catalyst technologies, technical service, or process engineering, with a strong understanding of refinery economics and customer requirements.
  • Proven track record of leading technical teams and delivering complex projects in a dynamic, customer-focused environment.
  • Deep expertise in FCC catalyst technology and refinery operations, including direct customer engagement and technical problem-solving.
  • Strong business acumen, communication, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to translate technical solutions into customer value and willingness to travel as needed.
  • Availability to travel ~30% of your working time (Western and Northern Europe).

WHAT WE OFFER

  • Healthy work environment: where your safety and health (both physical and mental) are always our priority
  • Attractive salary package: based on a fixed salary and a bonus based on performance
  • Flexible working model: you will be able to work remotely within the country and adjust your schedule
  • Flexible compensation plan: to take advantage of services such as Health Insurance, Childcare, Transportation Card, and Training, among others
  • Professional development: we promote continuous training and access to internal vacancies to keep growing at BASF
  • Investments for your future: with a Pension Plan, Accident Insurance, Life Insurance, and even the opportunity to participate in the Stock Program after one year in the company
  • Work-life balance measures: as well as financial assistance for the schooling of children
  • Sports sections: where you can connect with colleagues and play football, paddle, basketball, cycling, yoga, etc.
  • And much more
